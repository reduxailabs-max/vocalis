# Stage 6a — Voice-engine plumbing (Auto choice, Kokoro, fall-down chain): change log

Branch `worktree-stage6a-engine-plumbing`. Plan and per-task reports:
`.superpowers/sdd/2026-10-16-vocalis-stage6a-engine-plumbing/`.

Quick Reader and Document Reader now pick their voice through one shared
path: the user's choice (Automatic / Qwen3-TTS / Kokoro / Piper) becomes a
plan, and a chain tries each engine in order, falling to the next lighter
one if it fails. A new light voice, Kokoro, is installed and checked at
startup.

## What changed

- **Per-reader choice.** `quick_reader_engine` / `document_reader_engine` (default `"auto"`) replace the never-used `quick_tts` / `doc_tts`. Old config files still load (unknown keys ignored) and mean Auto. New Kokoro settings: model path, voices path, voice (`af_heart`).
- **Auto rule.** More than 4 GB graphics card, PC not stressed (load per core at most 0.85, free RAM at least max(2 GB, 15% of total)) and enough free graphics memory -> Qwen3-TTS; busy PC or small/busy graphics card -> Kokoro; very low free RAM (under 1 GB) or no graphics card -> Piper. Vocalis's own loaded Qwen3-TTS memory is credited back.
- **Chains.** Forced Qwen3-TTS -> Kokoro -> Piper; Kokoro -> Piper; Piper -> Kokoro. A failure shows "Primary voice unavailable — using backup voice". Piper is never loaded through `ModelManager`.
- **Settings window.** Three new drop-downs: Quick Reader voice engine, Document Reader voice engine, Kokoro voice.
- **Installer.** Downloads the Kokoro files (325,532,387 and 28,214,398 bytes) with a 60-second timeout; installs `kokoro-onnx==0.4.7` with `--no-deps` (it needs plain `onnxruntime`, which would break Parakeet's graphics-card support); `uninstall.sh --purge` removes the Kokoro folder; `pyproject.toml` lists Kokoro's three small dependencies.
- **Startup checks.** New Kokoro file check; now seven checks in order: Parakeet, Qwen3-TTS, Kokoro, ydotoold, OCR tools, onnxruntime graphics card, stale `~/.asoundrc`.
