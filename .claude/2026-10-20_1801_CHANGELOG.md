# Stage 6b — Gapless long reading: change log

Branch `worktree-stage6b-gapless-reading`. Plan and per-task reports:
`.superpowers/sdd/2026-10-20-vocalis-stage6b-gapless-reading/`.

Long readings (Document Reader, and now Quick Reader too) play as ONE
continuous stream: the next piece is made while the current one plays, the
sound never restarts between pieces, and the voice stays loaded until the
reading has really finished playing.

## What changed, by task

1. **Chunker** (`text/chunker.py`): sentence-aware, exact character spans (`chunk_spans`), then made linear-time after a quadratic first version.
2. **AudioPlayer** (`audio/playback.py`): `open_stream` / `close_stream` / `report_device_failed`; new shared test helper `tests/fake_output.py`.
3. **SpeechStream** (`audio/speech_stream.py`): one persistent output stream, bounded queue (3), 8 ms fades, silence on underrun, pause/rewind, resampling, 10 s stall detection. Fix round: slow device open is not a stall, paused is never a stall, stop during open, hook not called after stop.
4. **Voice held for a reading** (`engines/chain.py`, `model_manager.py`): `EngineChain(hold_engines=True)` + `close()`, `new_reading_chain()`, `make_downshift_handler()`; new `ModelManager.current_engine`. The plan's "close() releases once" was wrong (use count rises on every reuse); the chain remembers the held engine object.
5. **ChunkedSpeaker** (`features/chunked_speaker.py`): the shared producer loop; no `stream.stop()` after a clean finish; stop is a permanent latch.
6. **Document Reader** on ChunkedSpeaker; fix round: repeat read stops the previous reading, lost-resume race closed, orphaned speakers and dropped progress fixed.
7. **Quick Reader** on ChunkedSpeaker: long clipboard text is read in chunks; copied-text notice from a side thread; end reported first, then wait <= 6 s for the notice, then "Done".
8. **Docs and this change log** (no code changes).

## Known limits (accepted)

Voice stays loaded while paused; a stop at the same instant as a repeat read can order reports start-before-end; Quick Reader can show both "Stopped" and "Done" if a stop lands between `run()` returning and the end (13/300 stress runs, pre-existing window); an engine that loads but fails to synthesize stays loaded until the reading ends.
