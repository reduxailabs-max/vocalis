# Changelog — Stage 11: logo and dictation animation (2026-11-14)

## What changed (plain English)
- New red-and-black Vocalis logo, drawn with Remotion, used as the app icon, the setup window logo and the applications-menu icon.
- While you hold the dictation key, a floating pill appears at the bottom of the screen: a glowing microphone in pulsing rings with red sound waves on both sides that move with your voice. After you let go it says "Transcribing...", then fades. Turn it off in Settings (Appearance) with "Show an animation at the bottom of the screen while dictating".
- While the animation is on, the "Listening / Transcribing / Done" notifications are skipped; errors still notify.
